Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of eleven players on a field at the centre of which is a 22-yard (20-metre) pitch with a wicket at each end, each comprising two bails balanced on three stumps. The batting side scores runs by striking the ball bowled at the wicket with the bat, while the bowling and fielding side tries to prevent this by dismissing each batter (getting them 'out'). Means of dismissal include being bowled, leg before wicket (LBW), or run out. When ten batters have been dismissed, the innings ends and the teams swap roles.

The game is played in various formats, from Twenty20, with each team batting for a single innings of 20 overs and the game lasting a few hours, to Test matches, played over five days with each team batting for two innings. Traditionally, cricketers play in all-white kit, and a hard, solid leather ball is used. Many forms of cricket are played at international level; the most prominent being Test cricket, One Day Internationals (ODIs) and Twenty20 Internationals (T20Is).
